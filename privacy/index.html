Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

MeetingMate respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use MeetingMate.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or sign in to a MeetingMate account, we may process information such as:

  • Email address
  • Authentication provider information
  • Account identifier

Authentication may be provided through email, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In.

Meeting Content

When you use MeetingMate, the app may process:

  • Meeting titles and metadata
  • Audio recordings you create or import
  • Transcripts
  • Meeting summaries
  • Decisions and action items

Audio recordings are currently stored locally on your device and are not synchronized to MeetingMate cloud storage.

Structured meeting data, including meeting metadata, transcripts, summaries, and action items, may be synchronized to our cloud service when you are signed in.

AI Processing

To provide transcription and summarization features, relevant meeting content may be securely sent to third-party AI service providers, including OpenAI, for processing.

MeetingMate uses the OpenAI API. Data submitted through the OpenAI API is not used to train OpenAI models by default.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as necessary to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Record or import meetings
  • Generate transcripts
  • Generate summaries, decisions, and action items
  • Synchronize meeting data across your devices
  • Restore your data when you sign in on another device
  • Maintain and improve reliability and security
  • Diagnose technical problems

MeetingMate does not sell your personal information.

MeetingMate does not use meeting content for advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Account and synchronized meeting data are stored using Supabase infrastructure.

Access to synchronized data is associated with your authenticated account.

MeetingMate applies access controls intended to prevent one user from accessing another user's meeting data.

Audio recordings remain locally stored unless a future feature explicitly informs you otherwise.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Supabase

Used for authentication, database services, and cloud synchronization.

OpenAI

Used to provide AI-powered transcription and/or meeting analysis and summarization.

Apple

May provide Sign in with Apple and App Store services.

Google

May provide Google authentication when you choose Google Sign-In.

These providers process information according to their respective terms and privacy practices.

5. Recording Responsibility

MeetingMate allows users to record or import meeting audio.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary permission or consent from meeting participants before recording, uploading, transcribing, or processing a conversation.

Recording laws differ between countries and jurisdictions.

Do not use MeetingMate to record conversations where doing so would violate applicable law or the rights of another person.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local recordings remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application, subject to device backup behavior.

Cloud-synchronized data may remain associated with your account until it is deleted according to MeetingMate's deletion functionality and retention procedures.

Certain limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backup recovery, or other legitimate operational requirements.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

You may:

  • Delete individual meetings
  • Manage locally stored recordings
  • Sign out of your account
  • Request deletion of your account and associated cloud data
  • Contact us regarding access, correction, export, or deletion requests

8. Children

MeetingMate is not intended for children under the minimum age required to consent to digital services in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MeetingMate evolves.

When material changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date and provide additional notice when appropriate.
